# Separate program sponsorship from clinical operation

Hawlak can use an insurer or integrated-care program as the Program Sponsor, but it will not treat all insurance employees as members of the care team. Identifiable discharge records, medication images, and red flags are available only to the hospital and the authorized Integrated Care Clinical Team under the approved care arrangement. Licensed clinical roles approve medication matches and resolve red flags. Underwriting, pricing, claims, and general insurance staff receive no routine case-level clinical access. Program reporting for business users is aggregated or de-identified. This boundary supports care coordination while limiting secondary use, role conflict, and unnecessary disclosure.

## Consequences

- The product requires separate clinical-operations and program-analytics permissions.
- The pilot agreement must define the parties' PDPL roles, purpose, lawful basis, data fields, retention, transfer, and patient notice.
- The UI must display the role used for each approval and sensitive view.
