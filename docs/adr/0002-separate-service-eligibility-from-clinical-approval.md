# Separate service eligibility from clinical approval

The insurer can decide whether a patient is covered or eligible for the Hawlak service, but an authorized clinical role must approve patient-facing discharge content, medication images, medication instructions, warning-sign scripts, and transfer rules. Commercial configuration and pricing remain separate from identifiable clinical content. This separation lets the insurer operate and fund the program without allowing a pricing, claims, or benefits role to change required clinical information.

## Consequences

- Every sensitive action records both the user and the role used.
- The workflow requires an eligibility decision before generation and a clinical approval before delivery.
- Content editors can remove optional wording, but locked clinical facts require a new source version or authorized clinical override.
- Program pricing applies at the contract, product, or covered-program level rather than through ad hoc patient-level clinical pricing.
